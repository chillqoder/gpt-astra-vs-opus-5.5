# Dust & Thunder

A complete procedural run-and-gun arcade game. Open **index.html** directly in a modern browser. No install, build step, server, libraries, downloads, or external assets are needed.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move / climb / aim | WASD or arrow keys | Left stick / D-pad |
| Jump | Space | A |
| Shoot (hold) | J or Z | X / right trigger |
| Grenade / tank cannon | K or X | B / left trigger |
| Knife | L or C | Y |
| Enter / exit tank | E | Right bumper |
| Pause | Escape or P | Start |
| Mute | M | On-screen button |

Aim up while shooting; aim down in the air. Down crouches and reduces your hitbox. Hold Down + Jump to drop through a platform. Climb marked ladders with Up/Down. Shooting at close range automatically uses the knife. Touch a captive or shoot their rope to rescue them. The tank has unlimited machine-gun ammunition and a recharging cannon. Touch controls appear on touch devices.

## Mission

Cross Sunburn Outpost, the Old Quarter, Blackrock Pass, and Iron Citadel. Defeat ambushes to unlock the route. Explore rooftops for two hidden caches and rescue all eight captives. A checkpoint activates at the tank depot. Break the boss's armor to expose its core, then finish the machine. After losing all lives you have ten seconds to continue from the checkpoint.

Everything, including sprites, parallax scenery, animation, effects, and the adaptive soundtrack, is drawn or synthesized in code inside `index.html`. Audio starts after your first interaction. Best scores are saved locally when browser storage is available.
